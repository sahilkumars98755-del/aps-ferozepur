News & Achievements

Academics, sports, co-curricular — the year in review, plus our news archive.

🏆 Achievements — Session 2023-24

📊

Academics

100% result in 2023-24 with an API of 432.25 (Class XII) and 442.34 (Class X). Rankings rose from 23rd to 12th at Western Command and 4th to 2nd at 11 Corps level. Toppers: Sanskriti Yadav (X, 96.6%); Sneha Kumari, KumKum, Khushi Tanwar and Neha (XII — Non-Medical, Medical, Commerce and Humanities).

🗣

11 Corps English Debate

Overall 1st position (Juniors) at APS Tibri. Randip Singh (VIII) won 1st prize speaking against the motion and was adjudged Best Speaker of the Day.

⚖

Ethics Bowl

2nd Runners-up at the 11 Corps Inter-APS Ethics Bowl, APS Jalandhar. Mannat Gupta, Gaurvi Rathi, Akshat Wadhawan, Srishti Yadav, Avanthika and Prachi received cash prizes and trophies; Mannat Gupta and Srishti Yadav performed at Command Level, Chandimandir.

❓

Quiz & Tournaments

2nd Runners-up in the 11 Corps Inter-APS Quiz (APS Beas); 1st Runners-up in 11 Corps Football (APS Tibri) and overall 1st Runners-up in the Table Tennis Championship hosted at APS Fzr — with 1st Runners-up in boys' and girls' singles and 2nd Runners-up in mixed doubles.

🏸

Badminton & Boxing

11 Corps Badminton: Runners-up and 2nd Runners-up (mixed doubles). At district level: Varinder Singh (IX C) won the Under-15 District Championship; Jaipal Singh (XII) won Gold in Boxing and Bronze at State; Sakshi (XII) won Silver (singles) and Gold (doubles) in Badminton; the football team won Gold and reached State level.

⛵

Individual Glory

Samaira Tandon (VI) — the youngest girl sailor in the APS fraternity — won bronze at the 24th YAI Youth National Optimist Sailing Championship, Mysore. Mannat Gupta (XI) was Best Delegate (₹5,000 prize) at DAV Patiala MUN. Rakshita (XII) won 3rd prize in INTACH's Poem for Peace Challenge. 19 children of Class V qualified the Sainik School entrance exam.

🎖 NCC Activities

2024-25

Annual Training Camp

66 cadets of JD, JW, SD and SW attended the 10-day Annual Training Camp at NCC Academy Malout (9–18 May), participating in Map Reading, Drill, Firing, cultural programmes and more.

2024-25

NCC 'A' Certificate

All second-year cadets appeared for and successfully completed their NCC A Certification this session.

2024-25

Celebrations & Community Service

Cadets celebrated Earth Day with a tree-plantation drive and painting competition, participated in Kargil Vijaya Diwas with gratitude posters, led 'Swachhata Hi Sewa' clean-ups of the campus, bus stand and railway station, and paraded on Independence Day, Republic Day and NCC Day (22 Nov).

2024-25

Officer Trained

Ms Aarti Yadav, TGT Hindi, earned an A-grade in the PRCN course at Officers' Training Academy, Gwalior, and the rank of Third Officer — now serving as Associate NCC Officer.

📚 Seminars, Workshops & Orientation Programmes

May 2024

Teachers' Training

An in-service teachers' training programme was conducted for a week during the summer vacations (13–17 May).

Apr 2024

Orientation for Students & Parents

Orientation programmes at the start of the session apprised students and parents of the school's working — academics, CCA and disciplinary issues. A talk on parental support was held for parents of Board classes on coping with exam stress, and a career counselling session by TGTs and PGTs helped Class XI students and parents understand the streams they had chosen.

2024

Expert Sessions

A memory-championship session by a blind-folded vision expert from Wisdom of Mind (13 Apr); a Sainik School and Rashtriya Military School awareness session for Class V (16 Apr); Basic Life Support and CPR workshop by 307 Fd Regt (14 Aug); drug-awareness session by Mr Karan Nangla with a theatrical show by HQ 7 Inf Div (6 Sep); career guidance by Ms Pushpinder Kaur Wadhwa, Wadhwa Career Institute (9 Oct); and a soft-skills session by certified image consultant Mrs Sphurti Pathak (9 Nov).

2024-25

Counselling

School-counsellor workshops on Anger Management, Hormonal Changes, Health and Hygiene, Time Management, and Ethics and Moral Values.

🗞 News Archive

School Raising Day

Investiture Ceremony

On School Raising Day, the Investiture Ceremony for Vice-Head Boy and Vice-Head Girl was held, in which Manpreet Singh Saggu (XI-B) and M. Sushma Sree (XI-A) pledged to perform their duties diligently. Class VIII students delivered a speech on the history of the school.

Open Punjab State Athletic Championship

At the championship held at Ludhiana (21–23 Aug), Jobanpreet Singh (IX A) won Gold in the 4 × 100 m relay and Bronze in the 200 m race.

District Rural Athletic Meet (Under-14)

Jobanpreet Singh (IX) won Gold in 400 m and 200 m, Silver in the 4 × 100 m relay and Bronze in the 4 × 400 m relay. Amanpreet Singh (VIII A) won Gold in 100 m and Silver in the 4 × 100 m relay.

11 Corps Inter-APS Hindi Debate 2019-20

Overall First Position. Dev Shukla (VIII-D) stood First in Juniors (against the motion) and won the Best Speaker Award; Shiwani Yadav (IX-B) stood First in Seniors (for the motion); Srishti (XI-A) bagged Second Position.

Kargil Vijaya Diwas — 20th Anniversary

Sub Maj Kaushal Kumar Sharma, SM, Kargil war hero, graced the occasion and inspired students with his experiences. The programme included a heartrending speech by Khushpreet (X A), a thought-provoking poem by Anand (V-B), a patriotic group song and a Hindi role-play.

International Day Against Drug Abuse

Students, teaching, non-teaching and Group D staff wore anti-drug badges. A special assembly for Classes VI–XII featured a video, speeches, poems and the role-play 'Drugs — The Monsters'. Around 825 individuals pledged to live a drug-free life; a poster-making competition was also organised.

Annual School Journal 'Ferozenith'

The Annual School Journal 'Ferozenith' for session 2018-19 was released on the school website.

Mayank Foundation Painting Competition

At the competition at Gandhi Garden, Ferozepur Cantt (14 Apr), Adhiti Jha (VI-E) won First Prize, Manraj Singh (X-A) Third Prize, and Shreeya (VI-E), Akshat Wadhawan (VI-A) and Krishna (VII-E) consolation prizes.

Baisakhi Celebrations

A special morning assembly with a melodious song by the school choir, a speech by Anushka (V-A) and a spirited Bhangra by the tiny tots of the primary wing.

World Health Day

Poster-making, speech and poem-recitation competitions were conducted by the Station Health Organisation on the theme 'Universal Health Coverage: Everyone, Everywhere'.

District Marathon

Ankit Sharma (X) won 1st position among 3,000 athletes in the marathon organised by District Administration Ferozepur (31 Mar), receiving ₹3,000 and the winner's medal.

Blessings-cum-Farewell Ceremony

Held for Classes X and XII (22 Feb). The Chief Guest was Mrs Jasleen Sandhu, Chairperson, GAFWO.

Republic Day & Christmas

Republic Day was celebrated with a special assembly — speech, patriotic group song, choreography and dance. At Christmas, students enacted the birth of Jesus Christ with thrilling dance performances.

Rendezvous with APSites

A programme showcasing the talents and abilities of students through their club activities.

Disability Day

The school choir presented an inspirational song at the Golden Arrow Asha School on the occasion of Disability Day.

All India Speed Skating Championship

Sunny Sindhu (IX-D) won Gold in 1000 m and Silver in 500 m at the championship held at MLM Skating Club (8 Apr, age group 14–16 yrs).
