Welcome to APS Ferozepur

A rich legacy, offering education with a difference

The school came into existence in September 1955 as the Garrison Nursery School under 167 Infantry Brigade. On 19 July 1984 it was converted to Army School under the Army Welfare Education Society (AWES), and was formally inaugurated by Maj. Gen. Narahari, the then GOC 7 Inf Div, on 1 September 1984.

Today, APS Ferozepur is a multi-cultural family of over 1,800 students. We believe in personal expression, celebrate experimentation by every learner, and give individual attention so that learning is accessible to all. The curriculum is multi-dimensional — an academic programme of high quality together with a wide range of co-curricular and extra-curricular activities.

Our Mission

To nurture caring, committed individuals whose lives are governed by discipline; who aspire to attain excellence in all spheres, and contribute materially to the progress of the society, nation and world to which they belong.
