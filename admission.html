Admission Procedures

Admissions are open for Classes Balvatika to XII (all streams). Here is everything you need to know.

✔ Lateral Admission (no admission test)

The following categories of children will be admitted based on Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous school, without an admission test:

  • aChildren coming on Pass/Promoted TC from another APS/AS, to be admitted between 01 April and 31 July.
  • bChildren who have studied for ten academic months in another APS/AS can seek admission after 31 July. An evaluation test may be taken to facilitate academic coaching and allocation of streams/sections — it is not an admission test, and the child is allowed to attend classes within 48 hours of reporting.

📝 Admission Test (other categories)

  • 1All other categories of children (including Army children) not covered under lateral admission may be admitted through a test. The Principal's decision is final.
  • 2The standard of the test is of the level of the annual examination of the class preceding the one to which admission is sought.
  • 3Tests are held on all Saturdays except the second Saturday; results within 48 hours. A re-test, if necessary, may be given after seven days.
  • 4The class for admission is decided by the Principal within ten days of reporting to the school.
